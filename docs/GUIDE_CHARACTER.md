# The guide character: designed and animated by Muse Image

The guide that pops up in live scan was **generated by Meta's Muse Image model**, not drawn by hand, and its face is
animated by swapping Muse Image edits of the same picture. SAM 3.1 helped place the face patches.

## How it was made

1. **Design (Muse Image, `/v1/images/generations`).** Three candidates from one prompt template, on a flat green background:
   > A friendly young tech engineer guide: short messy dark hair, a cyan glowing headset with a boom mic, navy hoodie with a small
   > glowing cyan arc-reactor emblem on the chest, dark joggers, white sneakers. Full-body standing character, front view, arms
   > relaxed at the sides, feet visible, friendly closed-mouth smile, polished modern cartoon illustration with clean outlines and soft
   > shading, appealing proportions, centered on a perfectly flat solid pure green (#00FF00) background with no shadow, no floor, no text,
   > no green anywhere on the character.
2. **Expressions (Muse Image, `/v1/images/edits`).** The chosen picture was edited five times with the instruction
   *"Keep EXACTLY the same character, hair, headset, hoodie, emblem, trousers, shoes, pose, arm positions, framing, proportions, art
   style and the flat solid green background. Change ONLY the following detail and nothing else: …"* followed by one of:
   mouth open wide ("ahh"), small round "ooh", wide smile showing teeth ("eee"), gently closed smile, eyes closed in a blink.
3. **Placing the face (SAM 3.1).** SAM located the "mouth" and "eyes" on the base picture; those boxes bound the patches.
4. **Assembly (`scripts/build_guide_sprites.py`).** Green screen removal (with edge despill), alignment of each edit to the base picture,
   and feathered face patches. Only the patches change while the guide talks or blinks, so hair and clothes never flicker.
5. **Animation (`GuideView.java`).** The mouth picture follows the letters being spoken over the length of the audio. Breathing, bounce,
   squash and stretch, and lean are applied in code. It is a cartoon, not a likeness of a real person.

Total cost of the art: about eight generations at $0.01 each.

## Rebuild

```bash
python scripts/build_guide_sprites.py <folder with base.png closed.png ah.png oh.png ee.png blink.png>
```
Outputs `guide_*.png` into `app/android/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi` and `res/raw/guide_sprite.json`.
